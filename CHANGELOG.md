## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-24

### Added
- Hold ⌃⌥ to show a floating deck driven by absolute trackpad touches.
- Brightness, volume and custom sliders, and a numpad that types into the frontmost app.
- Cursor is hidden and frozen, and pointer events are blocked, while the deck is up.
- Menu-bar icon showing Accessibility, hotkey and trackpad status.
- Optional `Config/Local.xcconfig` for signing with your own team, so the Accessibility grant survives rebuilds.
